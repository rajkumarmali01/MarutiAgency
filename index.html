Our Process

From Farm to Your Kitchen

We follow a meticulous process that blends tradition with modern quality standards.

Crafted With Care & Tradition

At JP Masala, every spice goes through a rigorous 7-step journey. We partner directly with farmers across Rajasthan, Kerala, Karnataka, and Kashmir to source the freshest raw spices. Our manufacturing combines time-honored stone-grinding techniques with modern hygiene standards, ensuring every packet delivers authentic flavor and aroma.

🌾 Step 1 — Sourcing

We work directly with trusted farmers across India's finest spice-growing regions. Each batch is handpicked at peak maturity for maximum flavor and potency.

1

🔍 Step 2 — Quality Inspection

Every lot undergoes multi-point quality inspection for purity, moisture content, color, and aroma. We reject any batch that doesn't meet our stringent standards.

2

🧹 Step 3 — Cleaning & Sorting

Raw spices are thoroughly cleaned using advanced de-stoning, washing, and air-drying processes. Impurities and foreign matter are completely removed.

3

☀️ Step 4 — Sun Drying

Spices are sun-dried naturally to reduce moisture content while preserving volatile oils. This traditional step enhances flavor depth and shelf life.

4

⚙️ Step 5 — Grinding & Blending

Using temperature-controlled grinding, we ensure spices retain their natural oils and aroma. Blends are mixed in precise ratios perfected over generations.

5

🧪 Step 6 — Lab Testing

Every batch is tested in our in-house lab for purity, adulteration, heavy metals, and microbial content. We comply with FSSAI standards.

6

📦 Step 7 — Packaging & Dispatch

Airtight, food-grade packaging locks in freshness. Each packet is sealed with nitrogen flushing technology and dispatched within 24 hours of order.

7
